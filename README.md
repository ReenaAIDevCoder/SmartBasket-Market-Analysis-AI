#  SmartBasket AI

## Intelligent Shopping & Market Basket Recommendation System

SmartBasket AI is an end-to-end **Data Science and Machine Learning project** designed to analyze historical online grocery shopping transactions and provide intelligent product recommendations.

The system discovers relationships between products that are frequently purchased together using **Market Basket Analysis, FP-Growth, and Association Rule Mining**. These learned purchasing patterns are then used by a recommendation engine to suggest relevant products when a customer builds a shopping basket.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

In online grocery shopping, customers often purchase groups of products that have a strong relationship with each other. For example, a customer purchasing noodles may also be interested in sauces, while a customer purchasing bread may also purchase butter or jam.

SmartBasket AI learns these patterns from historical transaction data and converts them into an interactive recommendation system.
